I am a research assistant at MARS Lab, Tsinghua University, working with Prof. Hang Zhao since May 2026. I am also a master's student at SUSTech, advised by Prof. Max Q.-H. Meng.

我自 2026 年 5 月起在清华大学 MARS Lab担任研究助理，与赵行老师合作。我同时是南方科技大学电子科学与技术专业硕士研究生，导师为孟庆虎教授。

My research focuses on learning dexterous manipulation from human demonstrations, combining motion retargeting, imitation and reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer. I am interested in generalizable manipulation policies and robot foundation models, supported by force-feedback teleoperation and scalable demonstration collection.

我的研究聚焦于从人类示教中学习灵巧操作，结合运动重定向、模仿与强化学习、仿真到实物迁移；关注可泛化操作策略与机器人基础模型，并通过力反馈遥操作支持高质量示教数据采集。

I enjoy building things across the stack — from mechanical design and force-feedback hardware to learning pipelines and sim-to-real deployment. I also co-founded Halfmind Technology, where I helped take an E-ink task display from concept to production.

我喜欢贯通机械设计、力反馈硬件、学习流水线与仿真到实物部署的完整过程。我也是 Halfmind Technology（哈夫迈德科技）的联合创始人，参与将电子墨水屏任务显示设备从概念推进到量产。
